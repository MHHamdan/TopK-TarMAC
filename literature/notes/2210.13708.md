# MARLlib: A Scalable and Efficient Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning Library

**Key:** `hu2023marllib` — arXiv:2210.13708 (2023, JMLR).

**Authors:** Siyi Hu and Yifan Zhong and Minquan Gao and Weixun Wang and Hao Dong and Xiaodan Liang and Zhihui Li and Xiaojun Chang and Yaodong Yang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Unified MARL library: standardised env wrappers, agent-level algorithms, flexible policy mapping, built atop RLlib. Disentangles task properties from algorithm choice.

## Method (5 lines)

Framework — not an algorithm.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Reproduces baselines across MPE, SMAC, GRF, MAMujoco.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

RLlib dependency; opinionated config layer.
