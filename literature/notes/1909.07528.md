# Emergent Tool Use From Multi-Agent Autocurricula

**Key:** `baker2020hideandseek` — arXiv:1909.07528 (2019, ICLR 2020).

**Authors:** Bowen Baker and Ingmar Kanitscheider and Todor Markov and Yi Wu and Glenn Powell and Bob McGrew and Igor Mordatch

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Multi-agent hide-and-seek with standard RL produces an autocurriculum with six emergent strategy phases (chasing, blocking, box use, ramp use, ramp defence, box surfing). Suggests competition + complex env > intrinsic-motivation baselines.

## Method (5 lines)

Self-play PPO at scale on a Bullet-based physics arena.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Custom; transfer evaluations.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Compute-heavy; closed-source env.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Motivates open-ended large-scale MARL — useful framing.
