# Value-Decomposition Networks for Cooperative Multi-Agent Learning

**Key:** `sunehag2017vdn` — arXiv:1706.05296 (2017, AAMAS).

**Authors:** Peter Sunehag and Guy Lever and Audrunas Gruslys and Wojciech Marian Czarnecki and Vinicius Zambaldi and Max Jaderberg and Marc Lanctot and Nicolas Sonnerat and Joel Z. Leibo and Karl Tuyls and Thore Graepel

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

VDN factorises the team Q-function as a sum of per-agent utilities; per-agent argmax then maximises Q_tot. Mitigates the lazy-agent problem of centralised Q-learning under partial observability.

## Method (5 lines)

Q_tot(τ,a) = Σ_i Q_i(τ_i,a_i); per-agent networks trained by backprop through the sum; weight sharing + role information improve generalisation.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

2D partially-observable predator-prey, switch riddle, key-and-door.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Additivity is too restrictive for many cooperative tasks (QMIX, WQMIX).

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Sets up the linear baseline for value-decomposition that QMIX/WQMIX/QTRAN refine.
