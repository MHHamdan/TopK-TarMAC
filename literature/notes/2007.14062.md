# Big Bird: Transformers for Longer Sequences

**Key:** `zaheer2020bigbird` — arXiv:2007.14062 (2020, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Manzil Zaheer and Guru Guruganesh and Avinava Dubey and Joshua Ainslie and Chris Alberti and Santiago Ontanon and Philip Pham and Anirudh Ravula and Qifan Wang and Li Yang and Amr Ahmed

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

BigBird combines random, window, and global attention to obtain a sparse attention pattern that is a universal approximator of sequence functions and Turing complete, while scaling linearly in sequence length.

## Method (5 lines)

Random + window + global attention; sparsity pattern fixed per layer.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Long-range NLP, genomics; SOTA on several long-context tasks.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Sparsity pattern not adaptive to input; same theoretical guarantees apply.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Theoretical anchor for `sparsity preserves expressivity` claims.
