# AgentTuning: Enabling Generalized Agent Abilities for LLMs

**Key:** `zeng2023agenttuning` — arXiv:2310.12823 (2023, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Aohan Zeng and Mingdao Liu and Rui Lu and Bowen Wang and Xiao Liu and Yuxiao Dong and Jie Tang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

AgentTuning: high-quality agent-interaction dataset (AgentInstruct) mixed with general SFT data. AgentLM-70B matches GPT-3.5-turbo on unseen agent tasks while preserving general LM capability.

## Method (5 lines)

SFT on 50/50 mix of AgentInstruct trajectories and general instructions.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

AgentBench unseen tasks; preserves MMLU/HumanEval.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Closed-loop quality bottleneck of GPT-4-generated trajectories.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Reference for agent fine-tuning data strategy.
