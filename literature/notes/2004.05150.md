# Longformer: The Long-Document Transformer

**Key:** `beltagy2020longformer` — arXiv:2004.05150 (2020, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Iz Beltagy and Matthew E. Peters and Arman Cohan

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Longformer replaces full self-attention with a combination of a sliding local window and a small number of global tokens, scaling linearly in sequence length.

## Method (5 lines)

Sliding-window + dilated-window + task-specific global attention.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

text8, enwik8, long-document QA, classification.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Sparsity pattern hand-designed; global tokens require task knowledge.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Hand-designed sparsity baseline analogous to a fixed-k schedule.
