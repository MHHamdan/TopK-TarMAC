# Mean Field Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

**Key:** `yang2018meanfield` — arXiv:1802.05438 (2018, ICML).

**Authors:** Yaodong Yang and Rui Luo and Minne Li and Ming Zhou and Weinan Zhang and Jun Wang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Approximates many-agent interactions by the mean effect of neighbours/population; develops MF-Q and MF-AC; convergence analysed via Nash equilibrium of the induced game.

## Method (5 lines)

Replace joint-action arg in Q(s,a) by Q(s,a_i, ā_{-i}) with ā being mean neighbour action; iterate fixed-point updates.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Gaussian squeeze, Ising model, battle games (100s of agents).

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Mean-field assumption breaks down when local heterogeneity matters.
