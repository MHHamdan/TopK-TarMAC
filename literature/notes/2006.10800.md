# Weighted QMIX: Expanding Monotonic Value Function Factorisation

**Key:** `rashid2020wqmix` — arXiv:2006.10800 (2020, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Tabish Rashid and Gregory Farquhar and Bei Peng and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

QMIX's projection step minimises an unweighted error over joint actions, which can fail to recover the optimal policy even given Q*. WQMIX places extra weight on better joint actions; OW-QMIX and CW-QMIX provably recover the optimal greedy action.

## Method (5 lines)

Weighted projection: w(s,a) = α + (1-α)·1[a=a*]; α ∈ {fixed, learned}. Two practical variants: optimistically- and centrally-weighted.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Predator-prey, SMAC hard maps; outperforms QMIX, MAVEN.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Weighting heuristic; effectiveness depends on accurate identification of good joint actions.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Useful drop-in replacement if our method composes with QMIX-family mixers.
