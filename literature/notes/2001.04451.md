# Reformer: The Efficient Transformer

**Key:** `kitaev2020reformer` — arXiv:2001.04451 (2020, ICLR).

**Authors:** Nikita Kitaev and Łukasz Kaiser and Anselm Levskaya

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Reformer reduces transformer attention cost from O(L^2) to O(L log L) via locality-sensitive hashing of queries and keys, and uses reversible residual layers to drop activation memory from O(L) to O(1) per layer.

## Method (5 lines)

LSH attention bucketing + reversible residuals + chunked feed-forward.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

enwik8, imagenet-64; 64k-token contexts on a single accelerator.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

LSH approximation can miss rare long-range dependencies.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Hashed sparsity is a candidate baseline against learned top-k.
