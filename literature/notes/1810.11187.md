# TarMAC: Targeted Multi-Agent Communication

**Key:** `das2019tarmac` — arXiv:1810.11187 (2019, ICML).

**Authors:** Abhishek Das and Théophile Gervet and Joshua Romoff and Dhruv Batra and Devi Parikh and Michael Rabbat and Joelle Pineau

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Agents learn what to communicate and to whom via a soft attention mechanism over outgoing key/value pairs; multi-round communication improves coordination in partially observable cooperative tasks.

## Method (5 lines)

Each agent emits a key/value pair; receivers form queries; messages are aggregated by scaled dot-product attention. Multiple rounds before action; trained end-to-end with task reward only.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

SHAPES, MPE traffic-junction, House3D, Predator-prey; consistent gains from targeted vs broadcast comm, especially at high agent count.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Quadratic O(N²) communication cost; assumes a fully-connected communication graph; small numbers of agents in evaluation.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Direct ancestor of MARL-IoTP's attention communication; quadratic cost is the gap our scaling work would address.
