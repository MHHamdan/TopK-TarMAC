# Voyager: An Open-Ended Embodied Agent with Large Language Models

**Key:** `wang2023voyager` — arXiv:2305.16291 (2023, TMLR).

**Authors:** Guanzhi Wang and Yuqi Xie and Yunfan Jiang and Ajay Mandlekar and Chaowei Xiao and Yuke Zhu and Linxi Fan and Anima Anandkumar

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

GPT-4-powered lifelong-learning agent in Minecraft with automatic curriculum, skill library of executable code, and iterative prompting with environmental feedback; outperforms prior SOTA on item collection and tech-tree progress.

## Method (5 lines)

Three components: curriculum generator, skill code library, self-verifier.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Minecraft; 3.3× items, 2.3× distance, 15.3× faster milestones.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Closed-source GPT-4 dependency; Minecraft-specific.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Open-ended embodied agent setup, useful for grounding scaling claims.
