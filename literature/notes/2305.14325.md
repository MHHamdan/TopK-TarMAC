# Improving Factuality and Reasoning in Language Models through Multiagent Debate

**Key:** `du2023multiagentdebate` — arXiv:2305.14325 (2023, ICML 2024).

**Authors:** Yilun Du and Shuang Li and Antonio Torralba and Joshua B. Tenenbaum and Igor Mordatch

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Multiple LLM instances propose answers, then critique each other across rounds; consensus answers are more factual and improve reasoning.

## Method (5 lines)

K agents × R rounds of self-critique; majority/last-round answer used.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Math, biographies, GSM8K; gains over single-agent CoT.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

K·R-fold inference cost; no learning.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Inference-time multi-agent LLM scaling — relevant to distributed rollouts.
