# Categorical Reparameterization with Gumbel-Softmax

**Key:** `jang2017gumbel` — arXiv:1611.01144 (2017, ICLR).

**Authors:** Eric Jang and Shixiang Gu and Ben Poole

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

A continuous relaxation of categorical sampling that admits reparameterised gradients via the Gumbel-Softmax distribution, enabling gradient-based optimisation through discrete random variables.

## Method (5 lines)

Add Gumbel noise to logits, divide by temperature τ, take softmax; straight-through estimator for hard one-hot evaluation.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

VAE with categorical latents; structured-output prediction.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Straight-through gradient is biased; temperature must be annealed.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Provides the differentiable gate used in adaptive top-k routing.
