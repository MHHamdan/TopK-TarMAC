# On the Utility of Learning about Humans for Human-AI Coordination

**Key:** `carroll2019overcooked` — arXiv:1910.05789 (2019, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Micah Carroll and Rohin Shah and Mark K. Ho and Thomas L. Griffiths and Sanjit A. Seshia and Pieter Abbeel and Anca Dragan

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Self-play agents excel at self-play but fail with human partners on Overcooked; agents trained against learned human models coordinate better. User study confirms.

## Method (5 lines)

Overcooked-AI env + behaviour-cloning human models + SP/PBT baselines.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Overcooked layouts; self-play vs human-aware training.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Specific to two-agent cooperative cooking; behaviour cloning of humans is noisy.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Two-agent cooperative benchmark with low compute footprint.
