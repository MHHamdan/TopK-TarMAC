# Learning Attentional Communication for Multi-Agent Cooperation

**Key:** `jiang2018atoc` — arXiv:1805.07733 (2018, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Jiechuan Jiang and Zongqing Lu

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

ATOC learns an attention unit that decides when communication is needed and an LSTM-based communication channel that aggregates messages from selected nearby agents — improving large-scale cooperation.

## Method (5 lines)

Per-agent attention unit decides whether to initiate communication; a bidirectional LSTM communicates among initiator's neighbours; outputs thought vectors that condition the policy. DDPG actor-critic.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Cooperative navigation, predator-prey, jungle (custom).

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Locality assumption (neighbour set); LSTM channel is sequential.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Direct precedent for adaptive sparse communication.
