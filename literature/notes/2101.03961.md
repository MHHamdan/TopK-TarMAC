# Switch Transformers: Scaling to Trillion Parameter Models with Simple and Efficient Sparsity

**Key:** `fedus2022switch` — arXiv:2101.03961 (2022, JMLR).

**Authors:** William Fedus and Barret Zoph and Noam Shazeer

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Switch Transformers simplify MoE routing to k=1 and demonstrate trillion-parameter models that train 7x faster than dense T5-XXL at matched compute.

## Method (5 lines)

Single-expert routing + capacity factor + auxiliary load-balance loss + selective precision for the router.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

C4 pretraining, downstream NLP transfer.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Router instability requires bfloat16 / selective FP32; capacity dropouts hurt sample efficiency.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Top-1 learned routing at scale; gate-stability tricks are directly relevant to MARL learned-sparsity gating.
