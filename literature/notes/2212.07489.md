# SMACv2: An Improved Benchmark for Cooperative Multi-Agent RL

**Key:** `ellis2022smacv2` — arXiv:2212.07489 (2022, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks).

**Authors:** Benjamin Ellis and Jonathan Cook and Skander Moalla and Mikayel Samvelyan and Mingfei Sun and Anuj Mahajan and Jakob N. Foerster and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

SMACv2 procedurally generates scenarios and adds an extended partial-observability challenge, forcing closed-loop policies. Re-establishes a meaningful gap between SOTA and ceiling.

Benchmark — procedural unit composition + EPO observation mask.
