# Outrageously Large Neural Networks: The Sparsely-Gated Mixture-of-Experts Layer

**Key:** `shazeer2017moe` — arXiv:1701.06538 (2017, ICLR).

**Authors:** Noam Shazeer and Azalia Mirhoseini and Krzysztof Maziarz and Andy Davis and Quoc Le and Geoffrey Hinton and Jeff Dean

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

A sparsely-gated mixture-of-experts (MoE) layer routes each token to the top-k experts (k=1 or 2) of a large pool, enabling models with 100B+ parameters at constant per-token compute.

## Method (5 lines)

Top-k gating with noise + load-balancing loss; conditional computation.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

LM (1B-word benchmark), machine translation.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Load-balance tuning; gating noise required for stability.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Closest analogue to learned top-k routing; same gating-variance issue.
