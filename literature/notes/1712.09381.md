# RLlib: Abstractions for Distributed Reinforcement Learning

**Key:** `liang2017rllib` — arXiv:1712.09381 (2017, ICML 2018).

**Authors:** Eric Liang and Richard Liaw and Philipp Moritz and Robert Nishihara and Roy Fox and Ken Goldberg and Joseph E. Gonzalez and Michael I. Jordan and Ion Stoica

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Builds RL on top of Ray with hierarchical control; encapsulates parallelism and resource needs inside small tasks. Provides composable primitives for policy evaluation, optimisation, and exploration.

## Method (5 lines)

Ray actor model + abstract Trainer/Policy/RolloutWorker classes.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

A3C/IMPALA/PPO scale-out demos.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Opinionated API; debugging across Ray actors hurts.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Backbone of MARLlib; relevant for distributed-MARL infra positioning.
