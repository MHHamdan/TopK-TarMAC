# Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms

**Key:** `schulman2017ppo` — arXiv:1707.06347 (2017, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** John Schulman and Filip Wolski and Prafulla Dhariwal and Alec Radford and Oleg Klimov

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

PPO is a policy-gradient method that alternates rollout collection with multiple epochs of mini-batch updates on a clipped surrogate objective. Practical alternative to TRPO with simpler implementation and competitive sample complexity.

## Method (5 lines)

Clipped surrogate L^CLIP(θ) = E[min(r_t A, clip(r_t,1-ε,1+ε) A)] with r_t = π_θ(a|s)/π_old(a|s); k mini-batch epochs per rollout; entropy bonus.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

MuJoCo locomotion, Atari; matches/beats A2C/TRPO.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Sensitive to many implementation details (advantage normalisation, value clipping).

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Backbone of both MAPPO/HAPPO and modern RLHF — universal substrate.
