# HybridFlow: A Flexible and Efficient RLHF Framework

**Key:** `sheng2024hybridflow` — arXiv:2409.19256 (2024, EuroSys 2025).

**Authors:** Guangming Sheng and Chi Zhang and Zilingfeng Ye and Xibin Wu and Wang Zhang and Ru Zhang and Yanghua Peng and Haibin Lin and Chuan Wu

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Combines single- and multi-controller paradigms for RLHF dataflows; introduces 3D-HybridEngine for actor model resharding between rollout and training; 1.53-20.57× throughput vs SOTA.

## Method (5 lines)

Hierarchical APIs; placement strategy split across actor/ref/critic/reward roles.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

LLaMA-7B/13B/70B RLHF.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Complex placement decisions; framework-specific.
