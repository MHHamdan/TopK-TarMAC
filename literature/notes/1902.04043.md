# The StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge

**Key:** `samvelyan2019smac` — arXiv:1902.04043 (2019, AAMAS).

**Authors:** Mikayel Samvelyan and Tabish Rashid and Christian Schroeder de Witt and Gregory Farquhar and Nantas Nardelli and Tim G. J. Rudner and Chia-Man Hung and Philip H. S. Torr and Jakob Foerster and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Introduces SMAC, a cooperative MARL benchmark based on StarCraft II micromanagement, with decentralised partial observability per unit. Provides PyMARL, a reference framework with QMIX/COMA/VDN baselines.

## Method (5 lines)

Benchmark + framework — not an algorithm.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

14 maps spanning easy/hard/super-hard cooperative micromanagement.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Recent work (SMACv2) shows open-loop policies do non-trivially well.
