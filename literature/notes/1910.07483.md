# MAVEN: Multi-Agent Variational Exploration

**Key:** `mahajan2019maven` — arXiv:1910.07483 (2019, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Anuj Mahajan and Tabish Rashid and Mikayel Samvelyan and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

MAVEN hybridises value- and policy-based MARL via a shared latent variable controlling per-episode joint exploration. Addresses QMIX's restricted expressiveness and poor exploration.

## Method (5 lines)

Hierarchical policy samples a latent z each episode; QMIX is trained conditional on z; an inverse model + mutual-information loss encourages z to control distinguishable behaviour.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

SMAC hard maps where QMIX stalls.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Adds latent-policy complexity; benefits concentrated on hard-exploration maps.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Identifies QMIX's representational limit — relevant to value-mixer choice.
