# Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning is a Sequence Modeling Problem

**Key:** `wen2022mat` — arXiv:2205.14953 (2022, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Muning Wen and Jakub Grudzien Kuba and Runji Lin and Weinan Zhang and Ying Wen and Jun Wang and Yaodong Yang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Multi-Agent Transformer (MAT) treats joint-action selection as encoder-decoder sequence-to-sequence translation: encode agent observations, autoregressively decode actions in agent order. Linear complexity in N; inherits the multi-agent advantage decomposition guarantee.

## Method (5 lines)

Encoder: standard self-attention over agent obs → token embeddings + value head. Decoder: autoregressive action generation conditioned on the encoded observations and previously decoded actions. Trained on-policy with PPO-style updates.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

SMAC, Multi-Agent MuJoCo, Bi-Dex Hands, GRF. Beats MAPPO/HAPPO; few-shot transfers across agent counts.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Sequential decoding is unfriendly to inference parallelism; transformer is heavyweight for small N.
