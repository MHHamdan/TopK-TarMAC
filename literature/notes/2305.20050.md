# Let's Verify Step by Step

**Key:** `lightman2023letsverify` — arXiv:2305.20050 (2023, ICLR 2024).

**Authors:** Hunter Lightman and Vineet Kosaraju and Yura Burda and Harri Edwards and Bowen Baker and Teddy Lee and Jan Leike and John Schulman and Ilya Sutskever and Karl Cobbe

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Process-based reward models (PRMs) that supervise each reasoning step outperform outcome-only reward models on MATH; releases PRM800K, the complete dataset of 800k step-level human labels.

## Method (5 lines)

Train PRM as binary classifier per step; use best-of-N or RL with the PRM.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

78% on a representative MATH subset.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Heavy annotation cost; only verifiable-domain rewards.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Process-level credit assignment — analogous to per-agent credit in MARL.
