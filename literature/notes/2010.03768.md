# ALFWorld: Aligning Text and Embodied Environments for Interactive Learning

**Key:** `shridhar2020alfworld` — arXiv:2010.03768 (2020, ICLR 2021).

**Authors:** Mohit Shridhar and Xingdi Yuan and Marc-Alexandre Côté and Yonatan Bisk and Adam Trischler and Matthew Hausknecht

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Aligns ALFRED's embodied household tasks with TextWorld text descriptions; agents trained in text generalise zero-shot to grounded action.

## Method (5 lines)

Parallel text+vision environments; BUTLER agent with separate policies.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Text → vision zero-shot transfer; standard agentic benchmark.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Discrete action surface; relatively short horizons.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Candidate lightweight Lane-B benchmark.
