# Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model

**Key:** `rafailov2023dpo` — arXiv:2305.18290 (2023, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Rafael Rafailov and Archit Sharma and Eric Mitchell and Stefano Ermon and Christopher D. Manning and Chelsea Finn

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

DPO reparameterises the RLHF objective so the optimal policy has a closed form in terms of the reward; this enables training directly from preference pairs with a classification loss, eliminating sampling, KL control, and explicit reward modelling.

## Method (5 lines)

Loss = -log σ(β·(log π_θ(y_w|x)/π_ref(y_w|x) - log π_θ(y_l|x)/π_ref(y_l|x))); y_w preferred over y_l; β = inverse temperature.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

IMDb sentiment, TL;DR, Anthropic HH; matches or beats PPO RLHF.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Single-step preference assumption; can over-optimise the gap on rare pairs.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Lower-compute alternative to PPO for our agent-tuning experiments.
