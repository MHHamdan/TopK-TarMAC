# Dota 2 with Large Scale Deep Reinforcement Learning

**Key:** `berner2019openai5` — arXiv:1912.06680 (2019, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Christopher Berner and Greg Brockman and Brooke Chan and Vicki Cheung and Przemysław Dębiak and Christy Dennison and David Farhi and Quirin Fischer and Shariq Hashme and Chris Hesse and Rafal Józefowicz and Scott Gray and Catherine Olsson and Jakub Pachocki and Michael Petrov and Henrique P. d. O. Pinto and Jonathan Raiman and Tim Salimans and Jeremy Schlatter and Jonas Schneider and Szymon Sidor and Ilya Sutskever and Jie Tang and Filip Wolski and Susan Zhang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

OpenAI Five defeats the Dota 2 world champion via PPO + LSTM at massive scale (~2M frames/2s for 10 months) with a custom distributed system supporting continual training.

## Method (5 lines)

Distributed PPO on team self-play; surgery for arch changes mid-training.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Dota 2 vs Team OG; superhuman.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Compute cost is industrial; many surgical re-warming events.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Anchor for scaling claims and continual training relevance.
