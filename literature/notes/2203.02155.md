# Training Language Models to Follow Instructions with Human Feedback

**Key:** `ouyang2022instructgpt` — arXiv:2203.02155 (2022, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Long Ouyang and Jeff Wu and Xu Jiang and Diogo Almeida and Carroll L. Wainwright and Pamela Mishkin and Chong Zhang and Sandhini Agarwal and Katarina Slama and Alex Ray and John Schulman and Jacob Hilton and Fraser Kelton and Luke Miller and Maddie Simens and Amanda Askell and Peter Welinder and Paul Christiano and Jan Leike and Ryan Lowe

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

InstructGPT: SFT on demonstrations → reward model on rankings → PPO against the reward model. A 1.3B PPO model is preferred over the 175B GPT-3 baseline; improves truthfulness and reduces toxicity.

## Method (5 lines)

3-stage RLHF: SFT, RM training, PPO with KL penalty to the SFT policy.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

API prompt distribution; TruthfulQA, RealToxicityPrompts.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Reward hacking; expensive PPO loop; RM brittleness on out-of-distribution prompts.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Defines the PPO-for-LLMs paradigm; substrate for everything in Lane B/C.
