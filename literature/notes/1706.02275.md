# Multi-Agent Actor-Critic for Mixed Cooperative-Competitive Environments

**Key:** `lowe2017maddpg` — arXiv:1706.02275 (2017, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Ryan Lowe and Yi Wu and Aviv Tamar and Jean Harb and Pieter Abbeel and Igor Mordatch

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

MADDPG extends DDPG to multi-agent by giving each agent's critic access to the joint observation and joint action; an ensemble of policies improves robustness against non-stationary co-players.

## Method (5 lines)

Per-agent actor μ_i(o_i); per-agent centralised critic Q_i(x, a_1..a_n); off-policy DDPG-style updates with replay; ensemble policies sampled at training time to combat overfit to specific co-players.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Multi-Particle Environments (MPE) — simple_spread, simple_speaker_listener, simple_push, predator-prey-style tasks. Beats independent DDPG.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Doesn't scale beyond ~10 agents; sensitive to hyperparameters; deterministic policy gradients problematic in fully cooperative settings.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Defined the MPE benchmark suite — our likely first-stage testbed.
