# Trust Region Policy Optimisation in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

**Key:** `kuba2021happo` — arXiv:2109.11251 (2021, ICLR 2022).

**Authors:** Jakub Grudzien Kuba and Ruiqing Chen and Muning Wen and Ying Wen and Fanglei Sun and Jun Wang and Yaodong Yang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Extends trust-region theory to multi-agent: a multi-agent advantage decomposition lemma + sequential update scheme yield HATRPO and HAPPO, the first MARL algorithms with monotonic-improvement guarantees that work with heterogeneous, non-parameter-shared agents.

## Method (5 lines)

Agents update sequentially in a random order each iteration; each agent performs a trust-region step on the advantage decomposed conditional on preceding agents' new policies. No parameter sharing required.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Multi-Agent MuJoCo, SMAC; outperforms MAPPO, MADDPG, QMIX, IPPO.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Sequential update scheme limits parallelism; still O(N) trust-region computations.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Theoretical anchor for any heterogeneous-agent communication method we propose.
