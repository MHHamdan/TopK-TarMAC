# Efficient Memory Management for Large Language Model Serving with PagedAttention

**Key:** `kwon2023vllm` — arXiv:2309.06180 (2023, SOSP).

**Authors:** Woosuk Kwon and Zhuohan Li and Siyuan Zhuang and Ying Sheng and Lianmin Zheng and Cody Hao Yu and Joseph E. Gonzalez and Hao Zhang and Ion Stoica

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

PagedAttention manages the KV cache as fixed-size pages, eliminating fragmentation and enabling cache sharing across requests. vLLM achieves 2-4× higher throughput than FasterTransformer/Orca.

## Method (5 lines)

Virtual-memory-like paging over KV blocks; copy-on-write for shared prefixes.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

LLaMA-7B/13B/70B; ShareGPT prompts.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Block size hyperparameter; integration cost with frameworks.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Standard inference engine for RLHF rollouts (used in HybridFlow/OpenRLHF).
