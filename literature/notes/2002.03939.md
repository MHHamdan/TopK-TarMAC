# Qatten: A General Framework for Cooperative Multiagent RL

**Key:** `yang2020qatten` — arXiv:2002.03939 (2020, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Yaodong Yang and Jianye Hao and Ben Liao and Kun Shao and Guangyong Chen and Wulong Liu and Hongyao Tang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Derives a general expression of Q_tot in terms of per-agent Q_i and approximates the coefficients with multi-head attention over agent embeddings, yielding an attention-based value decomposition.

## Method (5 lines)

Multi-head attention over agent observations produces per-head weights λ_i^h that combine Q_i into Q_tot. Theoretical Taylor-expansion grounding.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

SMAC — outperforms QMIX, VDN, QTRAN on most maps.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Still monotonic-like under non-negative weights; assumes shared state access.
