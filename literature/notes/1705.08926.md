# Counterfactual Multi-Agent Policy Gradients

**Key:** `foerster2017coma` — arXiv:1705.08926 (2017, AAAI).

**Authors:** Jakob Foerster and Gregory Farquhar and Triantafyllos Afouras and Nantas Nardelli and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

COMA uses a centralised critic and decentralised actors; the critic computes a counterfactual baseline that marginalises out a single agent's action to attribute credit, addressing the multi-agent credit-assignment problem.

## Method (5 lines)

Single critic Q(s,a) shared across agents; per-agent advantage A_i = Q(s,a) - Σ_{a_i'} π_i(a_i'|τ_i) Q(s,(a_i', a_{-i})); efficient because the critic outputs |A_i| values in one forward pass.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

StarCraft I micromanagement (decentralised, partial observability).

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

On-policy, fragile in sparse-reward; eclipsed by MAPPO/QMIX numerically.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Origin of multi-agent credit assignment via counterfactual baselines — conceptually related to attention-weighted message routing.
