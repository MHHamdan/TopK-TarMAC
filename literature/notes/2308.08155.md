# AutoGen: Enabling Next-Gen LLM Applications via Multi-Agent Conversation

**Key:** `wu2023autogen` — arXiv:2308.08155 (2023, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Qingyun Wu and Gagan Bansal and Jieyu Zhang and Yiran Wu and Beibin Li and Erkang Zhu and Li Jiang and Xiaoyun Zhang and Shaokun Zhang and Jiale Liu and Ahmed Hassan Awadallah and Ryen W. White and Doug Burger and Chi Wang

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Open framework for building LLM apps as conversations among customisable agents (LLM, tool, human). Demonstrates wide applicability across math, coding, QA, decision-making.

## Method (5 lines)

Framework: ConversableAgent abstraction with chat-history-based message passing.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Case studies; no headline RL training.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

No learning loop; quality bottlenecked by underlying LLM.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Reference architecture for multi-agent LLM systems we'd train at scale.
