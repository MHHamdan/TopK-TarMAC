# QTRAN: Learning to Factorize with Transformation for Cooperative MARL

**Key:** `son2019qtran` — arXiv:1905.05408 (2019, ICML).

**Authors:** Kyunghwan Son and Daewoo Kim and Wan Ju Kang and David Earl Hostallero and Yung Yi

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

QTRAN drops VDN/QMIX's additivity/monotonicity constraints and instead transforms the joint Q into a factorisable surrogate with identical optimal actions. Provably represents a strictly larger class of MARL value functions.

## Method (5 lines)

Joint Q_jt + per-agent utilities Q_i; auxiliary loss enforces Σ_i Q_i(τ,a_i) ≥ Q_jt(τ,a) with equality at the joint argmax.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Multi-step matrix games where QMIX fails; SMAC.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Auxiliary losses fragile; QPLEX/WQMIX often outperform empirically.
