# SEED RL: Scalable and Efficient Deep-RL with Accelerated Central Inference

**Key:** `espeholt2020seedrl` — arXiv:1910.06591 (2019, ICLR 2020).

**Authors:** Lasse Espeholt and Raphaël Marinier and Piotr Stanczyk and Ke Wang and Marcin Michalski

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Centralised inference on accelerators with thin observation-only actors. Reaches millions of FPS; matches SOTA on Atari-57 3× faster wall-clock and cuts experiment cost 40-80%.

## Method (5 lines)

Actor sends obs to a central inference server; server returns actions; gRPC streams.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Atari-57, DMLab, GRF.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Network is the bottleneck for very small per-step compute.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Centralised-inference design directly relevant to LLM-agent rollouts.
