# BabyAI: A Platform to Study the Sample Efficiency of Grounded Language Learning

**Key:** `chevalierboisvert2018babyai` — arXiv:1810.08272 (2018, ICLR 2019).

**Authors:** Maxime Chevalier-Boisvert and Dzmitry Bahdanau and Salem Lahlou and Lucas Willems and Chitwan Saharia and Thien Huu Nguyen and Yoshua Bengio

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

19-level grid-world platform for instruction-following; expert agent stand-in for a human teacher; quantifies sample inefficiency of current deep RL on a compositional synthetic language.

## Method (5 lines)

MiniGrid-based environments + procedural language generation.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Sample-efficiency baselines per level.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Compositional but synthetic language; small action surface.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Lightweight grounded-language benchmark; small compute footprint.
