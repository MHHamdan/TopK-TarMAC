# Distributed Prioritized Experience Replay

**Key:** `horgan2018apex` — arXiv:1803.00933 (2018, ICLR).

**Authors:** Dan Horgan and John Quan and David Budden and Gabriel Barth-Maron and Matteo Hessel and Hado van Hasselt and David Silver

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Ape-X separates many CPU actors with their own replay priorities from a single GPU learner sampling from shared prioritised replay; massively scales DQN and DPG variants.

## Method (5 lines)

N actors compute initial TD priorities; central PER buffer; one learner samples.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Atari — SOTA in much less wall-clock.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Off-policy + stale priorities can drift; large replay buffer memory.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Asynchronous-actor design pattern reused throughout MARL.
