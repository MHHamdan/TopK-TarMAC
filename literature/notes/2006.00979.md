# Acme: A Research Framework for Distributed Reinforcement Learning

**Key:** `hoffman2020acme` — arXiv:2006.00979 (2020, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Matthew W. Hoffman and Bobak Shahriari and John Aslanides and Gabriel Barth-Maron and Nikola Momchev and Danila Sinopalnikov and Piotr Sta{\'n}czyk and Sabela Ramos and Anton Raichuk and Damien Vincent and L{\'e}onard Hussenot and Robert Dadashi and Gabriel Dulac-Arnold and Manu Orsini and Alexis Jacq and Johan Ferret and Nino Vieillard and others

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Acme: modular framework for constructing distributed RL agents with a small set of clean abstractions; reference implementations of D4PG, MPO, IMPALA, R2D2, etc.

## Method (5 lines)

Framework — Reverb-based replay, Launchpad for distribution.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Atari, DMC, DMLab baselines.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

DeepMind-internal flavour of distribution; Reverb dependency.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Alternative to RLlib for serious distributed runs.
