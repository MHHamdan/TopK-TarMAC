# IMPALA: Scalable Distributed Deep-RL with Importance Weighted Actor-Learner Architectures

**Key:** `espeholt2018impala` — arXiv:1802.01561 (2018, ICML).

**Authors:** Lasse Espeholt and Hubert Soyer and Rémi Munos and Karen Simonyan and Volodymyr Mnih and Tom Ward and Yotam Doron and Vlad Firoiu and Tim Harley and Iain Dunning and Shane Legg and Koray Kavukcuoglu

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Decouples actors from a centralised learner; introduces V-trace off-policy correction for the lag between actor and learner policies; trains a single agent across DMLab-30 and Atari-57 with positive transfer.

## Method (5 lines)

Many actors generate trajectories with stale params; learner does on-GPU updates with V-trace corrected targets v_s = V(s) + Σγ^{t-s} ρ_t δ_t.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

DMLab-30 multi-task; Atari-57; throughput >250k FPS.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

V-trace truncates importance weights; not Pareto-optimal for very off-policy data.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Reference architecture for distributed actor-learner — relevant to scaling MARL rollouts.
