# Rethinking Attention with Performers

**Key:** `choromanski2021performers` — arXiv:2009.14794 (2020, ICLR 2021).

**Authors:** Krzysztof Choromanski and Valerii Likhosherstov and David Dohan and Xingyou Song and Andreea Gane and Tamas Sarlos and Peter Hawkins and Jared Davis and Afroz Mohiuddin and Lukasz Kaiser and David Belanger and Lucy Colwell and Adrian Weller

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Performers approximate full-rank softmax attention in linear time and space via random feature maps (FAVOR+), enabling long-context attention where vanilla transformers are infeasible.

## Method (5 lines)

Positive orthogonal random features approximating softmax kernel.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Pixel modelling, LM, protein sequences.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Approximation error trade-off; less effective at small N.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Possible method for scaling agent-attention to large N without O(N²) cost.
