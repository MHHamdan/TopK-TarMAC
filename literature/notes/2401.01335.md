# Self-Play Fine-Tuning Converts Weak Language Models to Strong Language Models

**Key:** `chen2024spin` — arXiv:2401.01335 (2024, ICML).

**Authors:** Zixiang Chen and Yihe Deng and Huizhuo Yuan and Kaixuan Ji and Quanquan Gu

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

SPIN trains a single LLM iteratively to distinguish its own samples from human-annotated data; the resulting policy converges to the target distribution. Outperforms DPO with GPT-4-labelled preferences in some settings.

## Method (5 lines)

Iter t: generate y' from π_{t-1}, optimise π_t with DPO-style loss using (human y_target ≻ y') pairs; no external preferences.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

HuggingFace Open LLM Leaderboard, MT-Bench.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Self-play can collapse; requires high-quality SFT starting point.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Self-play LLM training: relevant to single-policy MARL framings.
