# CAMEL: Communicative Agents for "Mind" Exploration of Large Language Model Society

**Key:** `li2023camel` — arXiv:2303.17760 (2023, NeurIPS).

**Authors:** Guohao Li and Hasan Abed Al Kader Hammoud and Hani Itani and Dmitrii Khizbullin and Bernard Ghanem

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Role-playing framework that uses inception prompting to keep two LLM agents on task; generates large-scale instruction data through agent-to-agent dialogue.

## Method (5 lines)

Two-agent role-play with inception prompt; transcripts collected as data.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

Conversational datasets; downstream fine-tuning gains.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Role-play heuristics; no quantitative cooperation benchmark.

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Multi-agent LLM data generation precedent; related to self-play.
