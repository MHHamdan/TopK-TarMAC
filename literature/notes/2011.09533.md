# Is Independent Learning All You Need in the StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge?

**Key:** `dewitt2020ippo` — arXiv:2011.09533 (2020, arXiv preprint).

**Authors:** Christian Schroeder de Witt and Tarun Gupta and Denys Makoviichuk and Viktor Makoviychuk and Philip H. S. Torr and Mingfei Sun and Shimon Whiteson

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Independent PPO (IPPO), where each agent estimates its own local value function with no centralised critic, matches or beats centralised joint-value-function methods on SMAC with little tuning. Suggests robustness to non-stationarity matters more than centralised information at SMAC's scale.

## Method (5 lines)

PPO per agent with shared parameters and only local observations + actions — no centralised critic, no value decomposition.

## Benchmarks + headline numbers

SMAC v1; matches or beats QMIX, MAVEN, QTRAN, COMA on most maps.

## Limitations / open problems flagged by authors

Empirical; doesn't theoretically explain why IPPO works; sensitive to PPO implementation details (clipping, advantage normalisation).

## Connection to MARL-IoTP / target team

Pure-independence baseline against which any communication method must show value.
