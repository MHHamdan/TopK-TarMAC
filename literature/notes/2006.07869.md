# Benchmarking Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning Algorithms in Cooperative Tasks

**Key:** `papoudakis2021epymarl` — arXiv:2006.07869 (2021, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks).

**Authors:** Georgios Papoudakis and Filippos Christianos and Lukas Schäfer and Stefano V. Albrecht

## Abstract (verbatim or condensed from arXiv)

Systematic comparison of independent, centralised-PG, and value-decomposition MARL methods across a diverse cooperative test suite; introduces EPyMARL, an extension of PyMARL with more algorithms and configurations.

## Method (5 lines)

Empirical benchmarking; codebase release.

MPE, SMAC, LBF, RWARE; published per-task numbers.
