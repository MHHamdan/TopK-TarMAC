\subsubsection*{Broader Impact Statement}
This work is a measurement and correction study of a computational-efficiency
claim. It introduces no new capability and no new data. Its intended effect is
to reduce wasted compute in the design of multi-agent communication
architectures by discouraging optimisation against an operation count that does
not predict runtime.

\subsubsection*{Reproducibility Statement}
The kernels, measurement harness, analysis scripts and every measurement
artifact are provided as anonymised supplementary material. Two scripts
regenerate every table and figure from the artifacts; a third re-derives the
numbers quoted in the main text from a manifest that names each one's source
artifact and tolerance, and fails on any disagreement. The environment, commands
and measurement parameters are in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.
