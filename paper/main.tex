\subsubsection*{Broader Impact Statement}
This work is a measurement and correction study of a computational-efficiency
claim. It introduces no new capability and no new data. Its intended effect is
to reduce wasted compute in the design of multi-agent communication
architectures by discouraging optimisation against an operation count that
does not predict runtime. We note that a negative efficiency result of this
kind is specific to the deployment regime studied
(Section~\ref{sec:limitations}) and should not be read as an argument against
sparse communication in bandwidth-constrained settings, where the objective is
different.
