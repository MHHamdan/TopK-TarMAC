Attention-based inter-agent communication aggregates over all $N-1$ peers per
agent per step, and restricting each agent to its top-$k$ peers by attention
score is an appealing way to make that cheaper --- a case normally made by
counting operations rather than by measuring time. We built one and report
why we abandoned it, at three levels.

First, an analytic ceiling. In \emph{score-then-select} top-$k$ attention, peer
selection requires the complete score vector, so all candidate scores must
first be computed. Only the value-aggregation product --- one of the two equal
terms in the attention multiply--accumulate count --- is sparsifiable, and the
reduction is bounded above by $2\times$ for every $k$ and
$N$, not the $1-k/(N-1)$ the aggregation term alone suggests. Selection that
fixes the retained set before the scores exist escapes the bound: with selector
cost $C_{\mathrm{sel}} = o(Nkd)$ the count ratio is asymptotic to $N/k$, exactly
$N/k$ only when the selector is free.

Second, what executes. Applying top-$k$ as a \emph{mask}, as our first
implementation did, leaves the dense aggregation MAC count unchanged and adds
selection and masking overhead, while a FLOP model based on $1-k/(N-1)$ reports
a $75\%$ saving at our headline $N{=}512$, $k{=}127$ cell. We implement the
genuinely sparse gather instead.

Third, what it costs. Across a head-width $\times$ batch $\times$ $N$ $\times$
$k$ sweep on one GPU architecture the gathered path is slower than unfused
dense in all $46$ cells; the ordering also holds on a CPU whose machine balance
differs by an order of magnitude. A component decomposition and a roofline
analysis explain why: at $N{=}512$ the sparse path moves $3.8\times$ the
traffic for $0.6\times$ the arithmetic --- removing arithmetic from a
memory-bound kernel does not save time. A secondary pre-registered training
study establishes that the communicating architecture improves return; its six
sparsification contrasts are under-powered nulls at five seeds.
Constructively:
a successor method must economise on the selector, not the aggregation. We
release the harness, the corrected implementation, and a checker that
re-derives every headline number from raw artifacts.
