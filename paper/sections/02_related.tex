\paragraph{Cooperative MARL with CTDE.} Three families dominate the
cooperative MARL landscape: independent learning \citep{dewitt2020ippo},
centralised critics with decentralised actors \citep{lowe2017maddpg,
yu2022mappo, foerster2017coma}, and value-decomposition
\citep{sunehag2017vdn, rashid2018qmix, son2019qtran, rashid2020wqmix}.
\citet{kuba2021happo} extended trust-region theory to heterogeneous agents
via the multi-agent advantage decomposition lemma, and the Multi-Agent
Transformer \citep{wen2022mat} casts joint-action selection as
sequence-to-sequence translation. The method developed here is built on
MAPPO; the critic is unchanged.

\paragraph{Attention as communication.} TarMAC \citep{das2019tarmac}
introduced soft attention over emitted key/value messages. CommNet
\citep{sukhbaatar2016commnet} used mean aggregation; DIAL
\citep{foerster2016dial} backpropagated through a noisy continuous channel;
IC3Net \citep{singh2018ic3net} added a per-agent gate; ATOC
\citep{jiang2018atoc} learned a binary attention unit for whether to
initiate communication; DGN \citep{jiang2018dgn} treats the system as a
dynamic graph. None of these works reports an explicit FLOPs-vs-return
trade-off, and the soft-attention variants are all $\mathcal{O}(N^2)$ in
the aggregation step. Qatten \citep{yang2020qatten} places attention at
the mixer rather than the channel; the two loci are substitutes that are
not compared on a level footing in the existing literature.

\paragraph{Sparse attention outside MARL.} Sparse and approximate
attention for long sequences is mature. Performers
\citep{choromanski2021performers} approximate softmax with positive
orthogonal random features for linear-time attention. Reformer
\citep{kitaev2020reformer} uses locality-sensitive hashing to bucket
queries and keys. Longformer \citep{beltagy2020longformer} combines a
sliding window with a small set of global tokens, and BigBird
\citep{zaheer2020bigbird} adds a random-attention component to recover
universal-approximation guarantees. The MARL communication channel
operates at $N \ll L$ and pays for aggregation rather than score
construction, so a simpler hard-mask top-$k$ aggregation suffices and
preserves an exact reduce-to-baseline ablation at $k{=}N{-}1$.

\paragraph{Differentiable sparsity and learned routing.} The closest
prior work to a learned top-$k$ gate over a fixed pool of destinations is
the sparsely-gated mixture-of-experts (MoE) layer
\citep{shazeer2017moe}, which routes each token to the top-$k$ experts of
a large pool via a noisy gating function, and Switch Transformers
\citep{fedus2022switch}, which simplify the routing to $k{=}1$ and
introduce capacity-factor and selective-precision tricks to stabilise the
router. Both report router instability and required auxiliary
load-balancing losses, foreshadowing the gate-variance behaviour observed
in this study.

\paragraph{Distributed MARL infrastructure.} Centralised-inference
architectures including SEED-RL \citep{espeholt2020seedrl} and Ape-X
\citep{horgan2018apex} consolidate policy evaluation on accelerators;
RLlib \citep{liang2017rllib}, Acme \citep{hoffman2020acme}, and MARLlib
\citep{hu2023marllib} provide composable substrates. None of these
addresses the per-agent attention-aggregation step that this paper
targets.
